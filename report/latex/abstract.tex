\begin{abstract}
Monthly rent estimates help tenants and owners compare similar homes in Singapore's HDB rental market.  Prediction is difficult because test approvals mostly occur after the labeled period and identical observed inputs can have different rents.  We combine address normalization, rent cleaning and geographic features with a tabular BERT trained by masked-field reconstruction and rent regression.  On a future-month holdout cleaned before splitting, BERT achieves 468.34 SGD/month RMSE against retained original rents, compared with 468.11 for shallow CatBoost and 497.97 for a recent grouped mean.  This window shows little RMSE benefit from the more complex model, while separate baseline ablations with training-only cleaning identify the time group as the most influential tested feature group.
\end{abstract}
