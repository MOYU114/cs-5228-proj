\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We studied future-month HDB rent prediction through EDA-guided preprocessing, baseline comparisons and a two-stage tabular BERT.  In the historical window, BERT achieves 468.34 SGD/month RMSE and CatBoost 468.11, with BERT having lower MAE and absolute bias.  The recent grouped mean is already within about 30 SGD/month RMSE of the best model.  Strict baseline ablations identify the time group as the most influential tested group, while monthly bias, unseen-building errors and the 168.04 SGD within-input variation component explain limitations hidden by the overall score.  Strict BERT evaluation and a no-pretraining comparison remain the priorities for testing the value of the two-stage approach.
